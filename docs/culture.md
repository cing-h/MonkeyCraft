# The villagers' culture: a Civilization of Knowledge

The ape village is more than the crew's workplace: it's a small civilization that values knowledge. This page is the lore behind the decorations, trades and gatherings in the village (`ui/station/ape/culture.js`) and the culture lines in the chatter (`chatter-lines.js`).

Everything here is scenery, with two exceptions. The lanterns at the Ring follow real jobs, and the council (final-changes item 5, part 2c) is real work by the crew.

## The way of the Leaf-Ledger
The villagers keep a great shared record, the **Leaf-Ledger**: what was found, made and learned, marked in glyphs on leaves and scrolls. Its five principles:
1. **Be curious.** Go and look: the next branch may hold fruit.
2. **Keep careful records.** "The Ledger remembers what we forget."
3. **Share the fruit you find**, and say where the tree is.
4. **Give honest feedback, kindly.** "A branch that hides its rot breaks."
5. **Leave the branch as you found it.** Ask before you take.

## Places
- **The Council Ring** is the wide platform round the great tree on Canopy 1. The great trunk itself is the *talking-totem*, with three carved faces and glyphs.
  - Round it: woven mats, log stools, a fire bowl, glyph banners hanging from the boughs and a string of twelve gourd lanterns.
  - The council drum stands at one side.
- **Totems.** The finished totem by the Chief's ladder carries the faces of the Chief, the Scout, the Smith and the Writer. The carver is still working on the new one between the Inspector's workshop and the bathhouse, for the Inspector, the Sage and the Bell-ringer. Its top face is still rough wood.
- **The offering shrine** stands by the Inspector's workshop, with fruit and a scroll left for whoever needs them.
- **Bunting** of woven pennants, in the crew's colours, hangs between the great trees and along every rope bridge.
- **Gourd lanterns** hang from the boughs of both canopy stories. Woven **idea-nets** (knowledge-catchers with feathers) hang on the trees and at the Ring.
- **The Chief's lodge** has carved masks on its walls and painted shields on the edge of its platform.

## Rituals
- **The gathering.**
  - Every few minutes the villagers gather at the Ring, and any crew who are free may join them. Busy crew never do, and a job calls them away at once.
  - One villager drums, the fire burns higher, and they talk; the chatter often turns to the sayings of the Leaf-Ledger.
- **The lantern ritual.** One lantern at the Ring is lit for each job the crew worked on today, so the string of lanterns is a real tally. When a job finishes, the next lantern flares into light. They're all dark again the next day.
- **The council.** The drum calls the crew to the Ring for a real council: they review recent work, share feedback and ideas, and the Chief reports anything significant to the user.
